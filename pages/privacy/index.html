Privacy Policy

Jubro respects your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Jubro may process, why the information is used, how it may be stored and protected, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Process

The information Jubro processes depends on how users interact with the service.

Account Information

When a user chooses to create or use an account, Jubro may process information such as:

  • Email address.
  • Account identifier.
  • Authentication-related information.
  • Basic account information provided through the authentication method used.

Certain Jubro features may remain available without creating an account.

Job Tracker Information

Jubro processes information users voluntarily enter into their trackers. This may include:

  • Company name.
  • Job position.
  • Application status.
  • Date applied.
  • Job platform or source.
  • Location.
  • Salary information.
  • Job posting links.
  • Recruiter or contact details.
  • Interview schedules.
  • Notes.
  • Custom fields.
  • Tracker configurations.
  • Other information entered by users.

Users control the information they place inside their trackers. Because Jubro allows customizable fields, users should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive personal information.

2. How Jubro Uses Information

Jubro may process information to:

  • Provide the job application tracking service.
  • Create and maintain user accounts.
  • Save trackers and application information.
  • Synchronize information across supported devices.
  • Display tracker data, statistics, and dashboards.
  • Maintain and improve Jubro.
  • Protect Jubro against unauthorized access, abuse, fraud, or security threats.
  • Troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Respond to support or privacy requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Jubro should process only information reasonably necessary for these purposes.

3. Local Device Storage

Jubro may store certain information locally in the user's browser or device. Local storage may be used for functionality such as:

  • Maintaining job trackers.
  • Remembering tracker information.
  • Saving preferences.
  • Allowing some Jubro features to work without requiring an account.

Locally stored information may remain until:

  • The user deletes it through Jubro.
  • The user clears browser or application data.
  • The application is uninstalled or reset.
  • The browser or operating system removes the stored information.

Deleting browser or application data may permanently remove locally stored Jubro information.

4. Cloud Storage and Synchronization

When users create an account or use cloud synchronization features, certain account and tracker information may also be stored in cloud-based systems used by Jubro.

Cloud storage may allow information to remain available when users sign in again or access Jubro from another supported device.

Information may therefore exist:

  • On the user's local device.
  • In Jubro's cloud systems.
  • In both locations.

The exact storage method depends on how the user uses Jubro and which features are enabled.

5. Authentication

If users use an account, authentication services may process information necessary to identify the account and securely sign users in.

Jubro should not store account passwords in plain text. When authentication is handled through a third-party authentication provider, the provider may process information according to its own terms and privacy policies.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

Jubro may use trusted third-party service providers for functions such as:

  • Authentication.
  • Cloud databases.
  • Application hosting.
  • Data storage.
  • Security.
  • Infrastructure.
  • Technical operations.

Firebase may be used as part of Jubro's cloud and authentication infrastructure.

7. Sharing of Information

Jubro does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary:

  • With service providers that help operate Jubro.
  • When required by applicable law, regulation, court order, or lawful government request.
  • To protect the security, rights, or safety of Jubro, its users, or others.
  • In connection with a future merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of the Jubro service, subject to applicable privacy requirements.
  • When users specifically direct or consent to sharing.

8. Data Retention

Jubro retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide the service.
  • Maintain an account.
  • Fulfill purposes described in the Privacy Policy.
  • Protect service security and integrity.
  • Meet applicable legal requirements.

Locally stored information generally remains on the user's device until it is deleted by the user, browser, application, or operating system. Cloud-stored information may remain while the account or associated cloud data remains active.

Where available, users may request deletion of information associated with their account. Certain information may need to be retained for limited periods where required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes.

9. Data Security

Jubro takes reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against:

  • Unauthorized access.
  • Alteration.
  • Disclosure.
  • Loss.
  • Destruction.

No website, application, database, device, or internet transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their accounts and devices and keep login credentials secure.

10. User Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and circumstances, users may have rights related to their personal information, including:

  • The right to be informed.
  • The right to access personal information.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • The right to object to certain processing where applicable.
  • The right to request deletion, erasure, or blocking where applicable.
  • The right to data portability where applicable.
  • The right to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • The right to submit a complaint regarding the handling of personal information.

Some rights may be subject to limitations or exceptions under applicable law.

11. Account and Data Deletion

Users who want to request deletion of personal information associated with their account may contact Jubro through its official contact information.

Deleting cloud or account information may not automatically remove locally stored information from every device. Locally stored information may need to be separately removed by clearing browser storage or application data.

12. Children's Privacy

Jubro is designed as a job application and productivity tool and is not specifically directed toward children.

Users who are not legally able to consent to processing of their personal information should use Jubro only with appropriate authorization from a parent or legal guardian where required.

13. International Processing

Some cloud or technology service providers used by Jubro may operate infrastructure in different countries. As a result, information may potentially be processed or stored outside the user's country.

14. External Links

Jubro allows users to save or access links such as:

  • Job listings.
  • Employer websites.
  • Recruitment platforms.
  • Other external websites.

Jubro is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites. Users should review a third-party site's own privacy policy before providing personal information.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Jubro may update the Privacy Policy when:

  • Features change.
  • Data-processing practices change.
  • New service providers are introduced.
  • Legal or regulatory requirements change.

16. Contact Jubro

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, please contact Jubro through the official contact information provided on the website.
